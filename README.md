# Kevin Konye — Product Manager & Business Systems Analyst Portfolio

A high-performance, responsive editorial portfolio showcasing 8+ years of experience across Product Management, Business Systems Analysis, QA Engineering, and AI-assisted delivery in Fintech, Banking, and Digital Channels.

## Features
- **Light & Dark Mode:** Native theme switcher with persistence in `localStorage`.
- **Interactive Filtering:** Filter case studies by Product Management, Business Analysis, QA & Testing, Fintech, and AI Workflows.
- **Resume & Print Optimization:** Pre-configured `@media print` stylesheets for one-click clean PDF export.
- **Direct Connect:** One-click email, call/WhatsApp, and clipboard copy buttons.
- **SEO & Social Cards:** Configured OpenGraph meta tags for rich LinkedIn and social previews.

## Free Hosting on GitHub Pages (Step-by-Step)
1. Go to [github.com/new](https://github.com/new) and create a repository (e.g. `portfolio` or `<your-github-username>.github.io`).
2. Upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js` to the repository root.
3. Go to **Settings** > **Pages** in your GitHub repository.
4. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and folder `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
5. Your portfolio will be live at `https://<your-github-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/` (or `https://<your-github-username>.github.io/`) in 1–2 minutes!

## Linking on Your LinkedIn Profile
1. **Featured Section:**
   - Go to your LinkedIn profile and click **Add profile section** > **Recommended** > **Add featured**.
   - Click the `+` button > **Add a link** > paste your GitHub Pages URL.
   - Title: `Kevin Konye — Product Manager & Business Systems Analyst Portfolio`
   - Description: `Case studies, system integration specs, QA frameworks, and delivery metrics across Fintech, Banking, and AI workflows.`
2. **Contact Info Section:**
   - Click the pencil icon next to your name/intro on LinkedIn.
   - Scroll to **Contact info** > **Edit contact info**.
   - Under **Website**, add your URL and select category `Portfolio`.
